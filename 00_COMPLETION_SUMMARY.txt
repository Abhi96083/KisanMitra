═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                        THE SOLUTION EXPLAINED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Why It Couldn't Work Before:
──────────────────────────────
Browser security policy (CORS) prevents JavaScript from accessing
different origins (localhost ≠ 10.126.227.21).

Why It Works Now:
──────────────────
Browser requests Flask at same origin (✅ allowed).
Flask forwards to ESP32 as server-to-server (✅ no CORS rules apply).
Response returns through Flask to browser (✅ same origin).

Simple Analogy:
──────────────
BEFORE: You try to call someone in another country (blocked)
AFTER: You call a local operator, who calls that country (works!)
